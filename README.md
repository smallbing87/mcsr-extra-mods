# MCSR-Extra-Mods
Mods that make it different than regular speedrunning   

Instead of using them for cheating, usually they're used for practice or for categories that are not regular MCSR.   

# World Generation / RNG Modify    

## FSG Mod    
Filters seeds. Contains multiple filers, including SeedBank, BeachFilter, and ZSG.    

https://github.com/DuncanRuns/FSG-Mod   

For category: [FSG](https://www.speedrun.com/fsg), [DMCE](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_dmce), [PogLoot](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogloot)    

## AreEssGee    
Filters seeds and modifies RNG, and it's highly customizable through SpeedrunAPI.    

https://github.com/faluhub/AreEssGee     

For category: [ARSG](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1n5Z3qsWbQX_uImx-HvzGacRpa6FjT5USRBnDKcsQbQ0)    

## PogWorld    
Modifies structures and their generating rates to be the absolutely greatest.    

https://github.com/faluhub/pogworld    

For category: [PogWorld](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogworld)    

## LuckySeed    
Customizable RNG and world/structure generating.    

https://github.com/LoneCoder21/LuckySeed    

For category: N/A    

## BeachFilter    
Please consider using FSG Mod instead of this since BeachFilter was already included in it.    

https://github.com/DuncanRuns/BeachFilter-Mod    

For category: N/A    

## PogLoot    
Modifies RNG, including chest loots, mob generations, dragon perches, and more.

https://github.com/AbyssStudios/PogLoot    

For category: [PogLoot](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogloot), [PogLoot CE](https://www.speedrun.com/pogloot_ce), [PogWorld](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogworld)    

## Tyche    
Modifies RNG (what direction item drops, reduces mob generating, etc.) for SSG.

https://github.com/VoidXWalker/tyche    

For category: [JUICE](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_juice)    

## RNG Stabilizer    
Modifies RNG specifically for mobs.    
The above link is based on the below one, and it's newer, and likely better.    

https://github.com/VoidXWalker/rng-stabilizer    
https://github.com/VoidXWalker/SetSpeed-NoRNG    

For category: N/A    

# Miscellaneous    

## Icarus    
Let you spawn with an equipped elytra, and a stack of fireworks.    

https://github.com/DuncanRuns/Icarus-Mod    

For category: [DMCE](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_dmce), [PogLoot](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogloot), [PogLoot CE](https://www.speedrun.com/pogloot_ce)    

## InfiniPearl    
Let you spawn with an ender pearl that can be used for infinite amounts of times.    
The above link is newer than the below one, and is more likely to be accepted when submitting runs.    

https://github.com/DuncanRuns/InfiniPearl    
https://github.com/VoidXWalker/infinipearl    

For category: [DMCE](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_dmce), [PogLoot](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogloot)    

## HalfHeartHardcore    
Makes you spawn in hardcore mode, and you can't go anywhere more than half a heart.    
The above link is newer than the below one, and is more likely to be accepted when submitting runs.    

https://github.com/tildejustin/half-heart-hardcore   
https://github.com/DuncanRuns/HalfHeartHardcore-Mod    

For category: [DMCE](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_dmce), [PogLoot](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogloot)    

## Noverworld    
Simulates the player being the middle of a run while spawning.    

https://github.com/logwet/noverworld    

For category: [DMCE](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_dmce), [PogLoot](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogloot)    

## Netherless    
Let you spawn with 12 ender eyes already in your hotbar, so you don't have to go through the nether the entire run.  

https://github.com/faluhub/netherless    

For category: [PogLoot](https://www.speedrun.com/mc_pogloot)
